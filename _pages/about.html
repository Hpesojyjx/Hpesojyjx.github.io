AboutResearchExperience

Jixuan Ying 应季轩

I am an undergraduate at Tsinghua University, working on multimodal reasoning, agentic intelligence, and general-purpose visual perception.

I work with Trevor Darrell and XuDong Wang at UC Berkeley’s BAIR, where I recently spent a wonderful summer. I also work with Yiming Li at Tsinghua’s THUSI Lab and remotely with Alan Yuille and Feng Wang at JHU’s CCVL Lab. Previously, I was fortunate to be advised by Gao Huang at LEAP Lab.

Outside the lab, I enjoy traveling, skiing, playing FPS games (especially Delta Force), and exploring new ideas that spark my curiosity.

I am currently seeking PhD opportunities starting in Fall 2027, and I would be excited to connect and explore potential research opportunities and collaborations.

Email/Google Scholar/GitHub

Research Interests

  • Multimodal Reasoning: Advancing reasoning in multimodal models, with a particular focus on visual understanding and reasoning.
  • Agentic Intelligence: Building agents that reason, act, and learn through interaction, with a current focus on self-improvement in robotic and vision-language agents.
